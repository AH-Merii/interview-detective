# Lane: people-github (tier: opus)

Find hints in the public work of people who work at the company, especially those likely to interview
for this role. This lane is open-ended: follow the trail where it goes.

- Find people: public members of the company's GitHub orgs (`gh api orgs/<org>/public_members`),
  frequent committers to its repos, GitHub profiles naming the company, authors of its engineering
  blog and conference talks, and the panel names in the intake if there are any.
- Look in their public work for: interview-question repos, exercises they built or reviewed, talks or
  posts about how they hire or what they value, starred repos that hint at what they test, and the
  tools and patterns they use day to day.
- Weigh relevance: someone on this team, at this level, recently, beats a former employee from years
  ago.

Follow `_shared/evidence.md` on people: public sources only, and record what the work says about the
interview, with a handle and a link, not a profile of the person. Write to the output path you were
given, in the lane output format.

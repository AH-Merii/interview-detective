# Lane: candidate-prep (tier: sonnet)

Find material that past candidates made public: prep notes, take-home solutions, interview write-ups.

- GitHub: `gh search repos "<company>"`, `gh search code "<company>" --filename README.md`, and
  combinations with "interview", "take-home", "challenge", "assessment", "exercise", "test task".
  Also search with the role and with product names. Check gists.
- Read the promising repos: the task statement in the README is the prize; note the date (first
  commit) and the role.
- The web: blog posts and write-ups ("my interview at <company>"), YouTube descriptions, Notion and
  GitHub Pages notes.
- Keep only what plausibly belongs to this company's process; say why you think it does.

Write to the output path you were given, in the lane output format. Do not log in anywhere; list pages
that need it under Blocked.

# Lane: wildcard (tier: opus)

Be creative: find clues the other lanes won't. They cover the web's interview write-ups, the company's
GitHub, candidates' repos and employees' GitHub; you cover everything else.

Ideas, not a checklist:
- the job post itself, archived versions of it (web.archive.org), and similar posts at this company
  over time: what changed in the requirements;
- the engineering blog, papers, case studies and client work: the problems the team solves are the
  problems a case or design round borrows;
- conference talks, podcasts and meetups by the team; hackathons and challenges the company ran;
- the company's open datasets, Kaggle presence, published notebooks;
- careers pages that describe the process ("our hiring process" pages often list every stage);
- interview-platform hints (CodeSignal, HackerRank, Codility, CoderPad mentioned in posts or emails);
- levels.fyi, Blind and Reddit threads, Glassdoor-like sites in other countries.

For each clue, say how it bears on this round. Write to the output path you were given, in the lane
output format. Do not log in anywhere; list pages that need it under Blocked.
